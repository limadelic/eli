# Limits

## What

The numbers are a dial, the limits are hard. A lesson goes in only by a merge, an extract or a split.

## How

- A file: max 2 sections, max 9 lines
- A section: max 7 bullets
- A bullet: max 2 lines of 80 chars
- Refs: max 3 layers deep from SKILL.md
- Bullets only, never a numbered list
- A section too big: extract it to a sub file and link it
- A skill that can not fit: the silo has two jobs, split the silo
